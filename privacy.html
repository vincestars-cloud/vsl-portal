Privacy Policy

rclone vince · last updated October 3, 2026

What this app is

"rclone vince" is a private Google Drive connection used by one person, Vince Stars, to move his own video files between his computer and his own Google Drive folders. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

The app accesses files in the owner's own Google Drive, using Google's Drive API, only to download and upload those files on the owner's own computer.

What it collects and shares

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal information, and no data from Google Drive is sent to any third party. Files stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own computer.
